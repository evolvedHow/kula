# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** November 8, 2026

**Contact:** vish.ganapathy@gmail.com

## 1. What Kula Genealogy System is

Kula Genealogy System is a Google Workspace add-on for Google Sheets. It helps families build and manage a shared family knowledge graph directly inside a Google Sheet.

## 2. How your data is handled

**The add-on itself does not collect, store, or transmit your data to any external server.**

All information you enter — names, relationships, stories, lineage details, and any other content — is stored in the Google Sheet you choose to use. The add-on only reads from and writes to that sheet through Google Apps Script.

## 3. Sharing within your family or collaborators

Kula Genealogy System is designed for family collaboration. Anyone who has been given access to the Google Sheet by the sheet owner can view and edit the data inside it, including data added through the add-on. We do not control who the sheet owner invites or what those collaborators can see.

Please use Google Sheets' built-in sharing settings to manage who can access your family data.

## 4. Google OAuth scopes

The add-on requests only the minimum scopes needed to function:

- `https://www.googleapis.com/auth/spreadsheets.currentonly` — to read and write data in the Google Sheet you currently have open.
- `https://www.googleapis.com/auth/script.container.ui` — to display the add-on sidebar.

These scopes apply only to the current spreadsheet. They do **not** grant access to your other files, emails, contacts, or any other Google data.

## 5. Third-party services

The add-on does not use analytics, tracking, advertising, or any external servers. It does not share data with third parties beyond the Google Sheet itself.

## 6. Data retention and deletion

We do not retain any of your data. Because all information stays in your Google Sheet, you can delete, modify, or remove access to it at any time through Google Sheets.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
